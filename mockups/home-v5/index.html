FAQ · plain answers, no clicks

Questions, answered up front.

01

What kind of barbershop is Joe's?

Joe's Barbershop is a traditional men's barbershop in Bostonia, in the city of El Cajon, California. Joe Denesowicz opened the shop in 2020. Joe and barber Alex offer haircuts, hot-towel shaves, beard line-ups, and clean-ups. Walk-ins are welcome. The shop is family-friendly and cuts hair for kids and adults at the same chairs.

02

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk in any time the shop is open — no appointment needed. Hours are Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday 10am–6:30pm; Thursday and Friday 10am–7:30pm. If you'd rather lock in a time, book a chair online or call (619) 891-2775. Saturday afternoons typically run a 20–30 minute walk-in wait.

03

Does Joe's take cards?

Joe's takes cash. An ATM is on premises by the front door if you need it. Most regulars come in with a couple of bills; if you forget, the ATM is right by the chair. Tips go straight to the barber.

04

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes. Joe's is family-friendly. Children and parents are both welcome at the same chairs. Kids can be cut by either Joe or Alex. Same address, same hours, same prices on the board. Parents can wait in the shop while a child is being cut.

05

Where exactly is the shop?

Joe's Barbershop is at 723 E Bradley Ave, Suite C, El Cajon, CA 92021 — in the Bostonia neighborhood of East County San Diego. Look for the round Joe's logo decal on the glass front door and the suite-C placard above. Free parking out front.
