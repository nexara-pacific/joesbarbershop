## What a line-up is at Joe's Barbershop

A line-up at Joe's Barbershop is a 20-minute edge-only service for $20, cash only. Joe Denesowicz sharpens the existing hairline — temples, forehead, and nape — with clipper work, then finishes the visible edges with a straight razor pass. The beard cheek and neckline get the same treatment if the customer asks. The line-up does not change length on top and does not shorten the beard; it is the in-between service that keeps a recent haircut sharp. Customers who got a [classic cut](/classic-cut) or [fade](/fades) one to two weeks earlier come back for the line-up so the edges stay clean before the next full cut.

## What's different at Joe's

The difference is honest pricing on a small service. Some shops fold edge work into a $35 "premium clean-up" or refuse to do it as a standalone service. Joe's posts $20 on the board, runs the line-up in 20 minutes, and treats it with the same standard as a full haircut. Cash only, ATM on site, no card surcharge.
