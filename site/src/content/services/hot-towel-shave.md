## What a hot-towel shave is at Joe's Barbershop

A hot-towel shave at Joe's Barbershop is the traditional barbershop straight-razor service — $30 cash, 30 minutes, on the letter board before the chair. Joe Denesowicz and Alex run the service the way it has always been done: a hot towel laid over the face to soften the beard and open the pores, a full lather worked in by hand with cream, a straight-razor pass with the grain, and an optional second pass against the grain for the closest finish. The shave ends with a cool towel and balm. The service is a clean shave to skin — customers who want to keep the beard order the [beard trim](/beard-trim) at $20 instead.

## What's different at Joe's

The difference is the model and the chair. The hot-towel shave at $30 cash matches the haircut price — no "barbershop spa experience" surcharge, no premium tier.
