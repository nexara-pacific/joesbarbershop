## What a kids' cut is at Joe's Barbershop

A kids' cut at Joe's Barbershop is the same craft as the adult haircut, in the same chair, at the same $30 cash price. Joe Denesowicz has cut kids since the shop opened in 2020 — first haircuts on a parent's lap, school-age cuts that need to be done before Monday, teen cuts that match the fade or [classic cut](/classic-cut) the dad just walked out with. There is no separate kids-only setup, no themed chair, no kids' menu. Kids sit alongside parents in the same mahogany chairs the rest of the shop uses. A booster goes on the chair for the smaller customers. The 30-minute cut has buffer for breaks if the kid needs them. The price is on the letter board before the chair.

## What's different at Joe's

The difference is the family-friendly model. Many East County shops are appointment-only, men-only-coded, or kids-only — none of those models work for a parent who wants to bring the kid along on the same visit. Joe's is built the opposite way: kids sit in the same chairs as parents, the price is posted, and the visit stays simple. Cash only, $30 each, ATM on site. The Bostonia strip-mall location serves families from [Bostonia](/bostonia-barber), [El Cajon](/el-cajon-barber), Santee, Lakeside, and La Mesa — working-class East County, where a family-friendly barbershop is what people are actually looking for. 4.9 stars across 91+ Google reviews and 33 Yelp reviews, with "great with kids" being one of the most-cited review themes. Book ahead if you are bringing the family; walk-ins are accepted when the schedule has room.
