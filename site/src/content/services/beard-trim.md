## What a beard trim is at Joe's Barbershop

A beard trim at Joe's Barbershop is a full beard shape, neckline clean-up, and razor edge for $20, cash only. The 20-minute service starts with setting the cheek line, then defines the neckline below the jaw, trims length to the customer's spec with clipper guards, and finishes the visible edges with a straight razor pass. Joe Denesowicz and Alex treat the beard as part of the cut, not a separate add-on lane — the same hand-craft, the same finish quality. Customers walk in for a beard-only service or pair it with a haircut. The service runs about 20 minutes from start to finish. The price is on the letter board before the chair.
